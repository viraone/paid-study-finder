Stay safe and get paid

Never pay to join

Real research pays you. Anyone asking for a fee, your bank login, or to "cash a check and send some back" is running a scam.

Answer screeners honestly

Platforms compare your screener to what you say in the session. Mismatches get people banned from future studies.

Read medical consent forms

Higher pay in drug trials usually means more time, more procedures, or more risk. Ask questions before you sign.

Use a separate email

Survey sites and panels send a lot of invites. A separate email address keeps them in one place and out of your main inbox.

Small surveys add up slowly

Survey sites often pay under $2 a survey and can screen you out partway. Stack a few, cash out early, and save your time for higher-paying studies.

It counts as income

Research pay is taxable. Companies like Sago send a 1099 once you earn $600 or more from them in a year.
